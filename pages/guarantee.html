The OPUS Promise

Progress you can believe in.

Our Money-Back Guarantee reflects the confidence we have in meaningful, supported academic progress.

  • Personalised support
  • Clear progress tracking
  • Real accountability

What this means for families

Confident support, with real accountability.

OPUS Tuition is committed to meaningful academic progress. If your child does not achieve the agreed improvement within 12 months, we will provide a full refund of the tuition fees paid for the year.

01

Personalised learning

Your child receives a supportive learning environment shaped around their needs and pace.

02

Clear progress

We track academic development so families can understand how their child is progressing.

03

Open communication

Parents and guardians receive regular, transparent updates throughout the learning journey.

Eligibility requirements

A shared commitment to progress.

Homework completion

The student must complete all assigned homework, allowing for up to three missed assignments per quarter or per 12 lessons.

Full programme commitment

The student must complete 48 lessons over one year, with one two-hour lesson each week.

Improvement calculation

Improvement is assessed as a trend using the average marks of school-issued Weighted Assessments and personal lesson topical tests.

Our commitment

Learning support that stands behind its promise.

If there is no overall increase of at least 10 marks after one year, a full refund of the tuition fees paid for the year will be processed.
