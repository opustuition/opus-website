What we are looking for

People who can inspire.

We do not simply look for people who can teach.

We look for tutors who are passionate about helping young people, responsible, patient, encouraging and academically capable. Most of all, they communicate well, keep improving and are genuinely invested in their students.

Who can apply?

University students ready to make a difference.

We are currently looking for university students who can tutor Primary and/or Secondary students in Singapore. Tuition is fully on-site at the student's residence; tutors can indicate the locations they are willing to travel to.

Universities

Students from all universities in Singapore are welcome to apply.

Commitment

A minimum commitment of one year is required, with preference for tutors who would like to stay longer.

Subjects we are recruiting for

Primary

English, Chinese, Math and Science

Secondary Math & Science

Lower Secondary Math and Science, E-Math, A-Math, Pure and Combined Physics, Chemistry and Biology

Secondary Languages & Humanities

English, Chinese, Literature, Social Studies, Geography, History and POA
